Background

Research, industrial partnerships, teaching, academic qualifications and professional experience.

I am currently an Associate Professor of Computer Engineering at the University of Naples Federico II in Naples, Italy. My research focuses on embedded systems, with interests spanning hardware security and trust, efficient computing, and emerging computing technologies.

At a glance

Research

Hardware to Systems

My research encompasses the design and implementation of high-performance embedded systems, as well as security and trust across sensor networks, the Internet of Things, and cloud-connected architectures. I combine theoretical investigation with practical development in collaboration with academic institutions and companies in Italy and abroad.

Projects

Industrial Partnerships

My project activities involve numerous collaborations with Italian companies, particularly in the railway sector. As scientific lead, I oversee the design and development of safety-critical solutions, ranging from custom FPGA-based hardware platforms to real-time software systems.

Education

Teaching and Mentoring

My teaching activities cover Computer Architecture, Microprocessor-Based Systems, and Embedded Systems. I also supervise Ph.D. candidates and M.Sc. students in their research and thesis work.
