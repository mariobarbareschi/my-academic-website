Ph.D. course · Embedded systems · practical design

Embedded System Design: A Practical Approach to Architectures, Configuration and Coding Techniques

Programme: Ph.D. in Computational Intelligence

Academic year: 2023/2024

Credits: 3 ECTS · 15 hours

A practice-oriented module introducing ARM Cortex-M microcontroller architectures and effective low-level coding techniques, with exercises that connect embedded-system architecture to reliable applications.

Main topics

  • Architectures and enabling technologies for embedded systems
  • ARM Cortex-M register organization and calling conventions
  • C/Assembly integration and low-level code optimization
  • Safety-critical applications on ARM Cortex-M platforms
  • Hands-on development on ARM Cortex-M3 boards

Teaching approach

Prerequisites included basic C programming and computer architecture. The course outline specifies 15 hours of instruction and 3 credits.

Ph.D. course · Embedded ARM architectures

Industrial Embedded Systems Design with the ARM Architecture

Programme: Ph.D. in Information Technology and Electrical Engineering

Academic year: 2023/2024

Credits: 4 ECTS · June 2024

An advanced, hands-on course on the development of industrial embedded systems based on ARM Cortex-M microcontrollers, connecting computer architecture, low-level programming, performance optimization and safety-critical design.

Main topics

  • Industrial embedded architectures, memories and peripheral technologies
  • ARM Cortex-M architecture, Thumb instructions and Embedded ABI (EABI)
  • GNU GCC cross-compilation and C/Assembly-level optimization
  • Embedded operating-system fundamentals: context switching, scheduling and communication
  • Safety-critical design, redundancy, self-testing and Cortex-M Memory Protection Unit

Teaching approach

The learning activities used ARM Cortex-M3 development boards. Final assessment was based on a student-selected project and a short presentation.

Ph.D. course · Safety-critical railway systems

Safety Critical Systems for Railway Traffic Management

Programme: Ph.D. in Information Technology and Electrical Engineering

Academic year: 2019/2020

Credits: 3.3 ECTS · January 2020

A research-oriented module on safety-critical railway signalling and traffic-management systems, with a focus on fail-safe computing architectures, railway safety standards, and real-time embedded software development.

Main topics

  • Railway signalling systems, operational contexts and technology development
  • Fail-safe and 2-out-of-2 (2oo2) computing architectures; vital outputs
  • EN 50126, EN 50128 and EN 50129; software life cycle, V-model and MISRA C
  • Design and implementation of real-time systems on ARM Cortex-M3
  • Model-based engineering, verification and tool-assisted analysis using Simulink and Polyspace

Teaching approach

Hands-on activities involved real-time software development, cross-compilation, debugging and model-based design.

Course details · Computer systems

Architettura e Progetto dei Calcolatori

Academic years: 2023/2024

Methods, architectures and technologies for designing computer systems, including pipelined and parallel processors, memory hierarchies, I/O, virtualization, and edge/cloud architectures.

Course topics

  • RISC/CISC architectures, ARM and MIPS processors, pipelines and hazards
  • Superscalar, multicore and multiprocessor systems; parallel speedup and cache coherence
  • Memory hierarchy, caches, virtual memory and interconnection buses
  • I/O peripherals and device drivers, DMA, interrupts and programmable controllers
  • Microcontroller-based and SoC systems for industrial applications
  • Virtualization, hypervisors, cloud, IoT and edge computing

Course information

M.Sc. in Computer Engineering · Year 1 · Semester 2 · 9 CFU (2023/2024).

Teaching included lectures and practical exercises using ARM development boards. Assessment consisted of a written design-based examination and an oral discussion, including practical work.
