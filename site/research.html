Research profile

Research

From hardware design to trustworthy and efficient embedded intelligence.

My research investigates how computing systems can be made more secure, dependable and efficient, with a particular focus on embedded platforms and resource-constrained devices.

I work across the hardware–software boundary: from digital circuits and processor architectures to embedded applications and connected systems. My activities combine theoretical methods, design automation, prototyping and experimental evaluation, often in collaboration with academic partners and industry.

Focus areas

Research directions

Four complementary lines of work, connected by hardware awareness and system-level evaluation.

01 / Security

Hardware Security & Trust

I study hardware-rooted mechanisms for establishing trust in embedded and distributed systems. A central topic is the design and evaluation of Physical Unclonable Functions (PUFs) and their use in authentication, secure key management and attestation, including protocols for IoT and edge devices.

PUF characterizationHardware roots of trustSecure protocols
02 / Dependability

Reliability, Testability & Resilience

My work addresses the assessment and improvement of computing-system reliability, from hardware accelerators to embedded processor architectures. It includes fault detection, testability analysis and evaluation techniques for dependable execution, with particular interest in machine-learning workloads and safety-critical systems.

Reliability assessmentFault detectionTesting methods
03 / Architectures

Efficient Edge AI & Embedded Architectures

I investigate how intelligent workloads can run efficiently on devices with limited memory and processing resources. The focus is on architecture-aware inference, computational latency and resource usage, including the exploitation of processor features, hardware acceleration and hardware–software co-design.

On-device inferenceResource-aware executionArchitecture-level optimization
04 / Design methods

Reconfigurable & Approximate Computing

Another part of my research concerns FPGA-based design, circuit synthesis and systematic design-space exploration. I explore approximation techniques to trade numerical precision for efficiency, alongside emerging implementation technologies such as memristor-based logic and in-memory computing.

FPGA designApproximate circuitsDesign-space exploration
Research in practice

From methods to real systems

These research directions meet in applications where computing resources, security requirements and dependable operation must be considered together.

  • Embedded, IoT & Edge-to-Cloud Systems

    Secure and efficient computation for sensing platforms, connected devices and distributed architectures spanning the edge and cloud.

  • Railway & Safety-Critical Applications

    Research-informed design of safety-critical systems in industrial collaborations, including custom FPGA-based boards and real-time embedded software. In these activities, I serve as scientific lead for selected development projects.

  • Prototypes & Research Tools

    Implementation and evaluation through FPGA prototypes, experimental platforms and design-automation tools, including previous work on IIDEAA and XbarGen.

Research outcomes

Research contributions

Four recurring contributions connect the methods I investigate with the systems I design and evaluate. Publications documenting these activities are collected separately in the Publications page.

Hardware-rooted trust

I study how Physical Unclonable Functions (PUFs) can be characterized and used as security primitives in embedded devices. This work combines hardware evaluation with the design of authentication and key-management mechanisms suitable for constrained platforms.

Hardware characterization · Authentication · Key management

Dependability across abstraction levels

I develop and apply approaches for assessing the reliability and testability of embedded computing platforms and specialized accelerators. The goal is to connect faults and architectural behaviour with their effects on application-level operation.

Fault effects · Testability · Reliability assessment

Architecture-aware embedded intelligence

I investigate how AI inference can be mapped efficiently onto resource-constrained hardware. By studying architectural features, execution kernels and hardware–software interactions, this work explores trade-offs in memory use, latency and computational resources.

On-device inference · Embedded architectures · Efficiency

Design-space exploration for efficient hardware

I explore methods and tools for comparing alternative circuit implementations, from FPGA-based and approximate computing to emerging devices such as memristors. This work investigates trade-offs among implementation complexity, performance and computational accuracy.

Reconfigurable design · Approximation · Emerging technologies
